Case study — Local business

Awishka Studio.

A wedding, portrait and event photography business in Panadura, repositioned from "a photographer with a phone number" to a studio worth booking in advance.

SectorPhotography / events
FocusPositioning, social proof, booking conversion
Live siteavishkatest.netlify.app ↗

The brief.

A local photography business selling a genuinely premium service — weddings, portraits, events — through a generic template doesn't look premium. It looks like every other local vendor with a Google listing. The site needed to close that gap between the quality of the work and the quality of the first impression.

Editorial warmth

A golden-hour palette, a serif-and-script headline pairing, and a full-bleed photograph as the hero — "Your story, held in golden light" sets the emotional register before any service is listed.

Social proof, upfront

The 4.8 rating and 21 client reviews sit above the fold, not buried in a testimonials section three scrolls down — trust signals work best where the hesitation actually happens.

Frictionless booking

WhatsApp and a direct phone number, both one tap away in the hero. For a local, relationship-driven service, removing the form-and-wait-for-email step is the whole conversion strategy.

The approach.

The stats bar — average rating, review count, shoot types offered, daily availability — does the same job a testimonial section does, but scannable in under a second. It's proof density, not proof volume.

A large ghosted signature and category watermark sit behind the fold as texture, reinforcing "wedding photography" as the category without repeating the words in the copy itself.
